# VidyGuideAI V3 - Vision Statement

VidyGuideAI is designed to democratize career counseling for Indian students and young professionals by providing localized, context-rich, and scalable AI mentorship.

---

## 1. The Core Problem
In India, high-quality career guidance is a luxury.
* **Counselor Shortage**: Over 90% of Indian schools lack dedicated career counselors.
* **Generic Recommendations**: Existing platforms recommend careers (like "AI researcher" or "investment banker") that are misaligned with local contexts, diplomas, or trade certificates.
* **Landscape Complexity**: Navigating Indian streams (Class 10 streams, ITI certificates, state-level entrance exams, SSC, UPSC, and local IT service company hiring patterns) requires deep localized data.

---

## 2. The VidyGuideAI Solution
VidyGuideAI scales expert mentorship using highly contextual Prompt Engineering pipelines and lightweight LLMs. It analyzes qualifications, maps them directly to the Indian industry and government exams, and outputs:
1. **Interactive Timelines**: Structured milestone sequences (e.g. from Class 10 to a lateral entry Diploma, then to a state government engineering post).
2. **ATS-Scored Resumes**: Plaintext builds targeting specific corporations (like TCS or Infosys) alongside PDF scanner analyzers.
3. **Conversational Support**: Multilingual guidance in regional languages.
